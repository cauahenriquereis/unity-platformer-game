# The Last Stand of Gaya

A 2D action platformer developed in Unity during high school, featuring
combat mechanics, enemy AI, a dialogue system, and an original lore.

## 📖 Lore Summary

**The World of Gaya**
Before the Parthenon, nothing existed — until supernatural forces created
the Gods, who shaped planets, universes, and life itself. A mysterious
planet rich in a unique energy called **Mana** became the target of divine
ambition, divided among the most prosperous Gods into **Continents**.

**The Conflict**
The Goddess **Gaya**, chosen as the progenitor of her Continent for her
connection to nature and creation, faced the wrath of **Amon**, the God
of Chaos, who desired control over the planet's **Mana Core**. Their
confrontation split the continent into two blocks — **Southgard** and
**Northgard** — and forced Gaya to seal Amon's body deep within the
planet, sacrificing herself in the process by transforming into the great
tree **Yggdrasil**.

**The Chosen Boy**
Five years later, **Kayn**, son of Raaz Hajrudin, comes of age and sets
out on the **Ritual of Adulthood** — a tradition where young warriors
face the **Ravine of Beasts** to prove their worth. Beyond the ritual,
Kayn carries a personal mission: finding answers about his mother, who
disappeared seven years ago.

**The Journey**
Armed with a legendary axe forged from **Dragonsteel** — a rare metal
born from frozen dragon blood — Kayn fights through corrupted beasts,
ancient traps, and dark caverns. Alongside **Hadúr**, a dwarven blacksmith
he rescues along the way, Kayn reaches Yggdrasil, where long-lost
memories begin to surface.

**The Truth**
At the great tree, Kayn discovers that Gaya is his mother. In a final act
of love, she condenses the Yggdrasil into a single branch — the **Lerad**
— so Hadúr can forge Kayn a new axe powerful enough to face Amon. Gaya
transcends, leaving her energy within the axe's gem to guide her son.

**The Final Stand**
Kayn descends to the depths of the Ravine, confronts Amon in a crumbling
castle, and defeats the God of Chaos before the seal is broken completely.
He then uses his axe to seal the crack in the Mana Core, stabilizing the
world — and returns home to confront his father, Raaz, whose secrets have
only begun to unravel.
